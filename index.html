Our story

More than just coffee

Born out of a love for great coffee and good conversation, Brew & Co started as a small corner kiosk and grew into Mangaluru's favourite specialty café.

We source single-origin beans directly from farms in Coorg and Chikmagalur, roast in-house every week, and brew with precision. Every cup is our craft.
